    🗣️ Speaking assessment

    Now speak in Dutch

    You'll complete a series of short speaking tasks. Your speech will be transcribed and evaluated for your Dutch speaking ability.

    How this works

    Read the task, press Start recording, speak naturally in Dutch, then stop the recording when you're finished.

    If a task is too difficult and you cannot answer it, you can skip it. This helps us estimate your current speaking level.
